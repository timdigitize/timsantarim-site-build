Our Brands

Annovi Reverberi

Italian diaphragm pump manufacturer

Founded in 1958 in Modena, in the heart of the Italian engineering district, Annovi Reverberi is a modern, flexible and constantly expanding company. In a know-how that has its roots in the heritage and personal experience of its founder Corrado Reverberi, in the talented people who work there, in the plurality and excellence of professional skills always involved. An international leader in the field of diaphragm pumps for agriculture and piston pumps for industrial washing.

Arag

Italian plastic accessories and electronics manufacturer

Established in 1976, Arag is now worldwide recognized as the main reference point in the spraying accessories field and in the precision farming area. Arag's catalog boasts more than 12,000 items and offers a complete choice without equal in the agricultural market. Within Arag's main designed solutions, a key rule is represented by GPS and computer monitoring systems, nozzles, lids, valves, filters, and several accessories.

Braglia

Italian brass nozzle manufacturer

Braglia Srl is an esteemed Italian company, recognized for its superior agricultural spraying equipment since its establishment in the mid-20th century. Specializing in precision agricultural sprayers, nozzles, and accessories.

Bondioli & Pavesi

Italian gearbox & shafts manufacturer

The credibility and reputation gained over almost seventy years of activity and market presence have made Bondioli & Pavesi a leader in the production of components for power transmission. The parent company of an ever-growing Group, Bondioli & Pavesi is a point of reference across the world in the manufacture of cardan drivelines for the agricultural sector and of gearboxes used in all types of industrial and agricultural applications, fulfilling the requirements of customers in every corner of the globe.

Agrotop

German precision nozzle tip manufacturer

Agrotop is a company known for specializing in the development and production of high-quality nozzle tips for agricultural spraying applications. Their nozzle tips are designed to meet a wide range of spraying needs, including pest and disease control, weed management, and crop fertilization, ensuring optimal distribution and minimal drift to improve overall crop protection and yield.

Tim

Tim Dijital Teknolojiler A.Ş.

Enhances the efficiency and effectiveness of agricultural equipment through electric control boxes, rate control computers, variable rate computers, and ISOBUS integration solutions. Electric control boxes facilitate easy management of equipment functions, while rate control computers enable accurate applications independent of speed. Variable rate computers offer more precise applications and prevent resource waste with its navigation features. ISOBUS solutions ensure different branded equipment operate in harmony, thereby increasing the sustainability and profitability of farming operations.
